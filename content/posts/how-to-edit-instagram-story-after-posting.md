<h2>What you can change in the 15-minute window</h2>
<p>Text sits at the center of this update. MacObserver reported captions and written words can be corrected after sharing, so wrong names, places or dates can be fixed in place. Readers then see the <strong>corrected wording</strong> on the same Story.</p>
<p>Music choices can also be swapped during the window. Reporting from GSMArena listed music among the editable parts of a Story. Picking a different track no longer needs a fresh post, because sound changes apply to the Story people already opened earlier.</p>
<p>Stickers and drawings complete the editable set. Gizmodo named stickers as part of the change, and PetaPixel included drawings in its account of the announcement as well. Moving, replacing or removing those items stays possible for a short time.</p>
<p>Think of the window as a grace period, not a second draft stage. Small fixes fit well here. Large rebuilds need fresh Stories. Plain mistakes get fixed fast. No drama.</p>
<p>Examples help here. A misspelled name in a caption counts as a <strong>text fix</strong>. A song that clashes with the mood counts as a <strong>music fix</strong>. Both problems have quick repairs now. The window allows it.</p>
