<div class="key"><b>In short:</b> To block a number on Android, open the Phone app, tap a recent call from that number, and tap Block or report spam. On a Samsung phone, open the Phone app, tap More options, then Settings, then Block numbers, and add the number. The call is then declined or sent to voicemail, and the number stays blocked until you remove it.<ul><li>Block from your call history: tap the call, then Block or report spam.</li><li>Samsung owners use Phone, More options, Settings, then Block numbers.</li><li>Turn on Unknown to stop private and unidentified numbers.</li><li>Unblock from the same list, and use your carrier tools for spam.</li></ul></div>
<h2>How to block a number on Android</h2>
<p>Blocking a number on Android takes less than a minute, and you do it inside the Phone app. Most Pixel, Motorola, and many other Android phones use the Phone app by Google. These steps come from Google&#x27;s own help page. They need a modern Phone app. Most phones running Android 6.0 or later have it. Menu names vary by phone maker, Android version, and carrier.</p>
<p>Start from a call you already received. That is the fastest route, because the number sits in your call history and you do not have to type it. Open the Phone app, tap More, the three dots in the corner, then tap Call history. Find the call from the number you want to block and tap it. Options open in a small row under the call.</p>
<p>Choose Block or report spam and confirm. Google asks whether you want to report the call as spam. You can block a number without reporting it, so answer that part however you like. For a personal number you never want to hear from again, the block alone is enough. Report a robocall or a scam call, because the report helps Google flag that number for other people.</p>
<p>After that, your phone declines calls from that number on its own. It never rings, and the block stays until you remove it.</p>
<ol class="steps"><li>Open the Phone app.</li><li>Choose More, the three dots, then Call history.</li><li>Select the call from the number you want to block.</li><li>Choose Block or report spam.</li><li>Confirm the block, and choose whether to report it as spam.</li></ol>
<div class="note"><b>Tip:</b> You can block a number without reporting it as spam. Report it only when the call was spam or a scam.</div>

<h2>Block a number on a Samsung Galaxy phone</h2>
<figure class="fig"><img src="https://tse3.mm.bing.net/th?q=Samsung+Galaxy+phone+showing+settings+menu&amp;w=560&amp;h=380&amp;c=7&amp;rs=1&amp;p=0&amp;o=5&amp;pid=1.7" alt="Samsung Galaxy phone showing settings menu" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
<p>Samsung Galaxy phones use Samsung&#x27;s own Phone app, so the menus look different from a Pixel. Both phones keep a blocked numbers list inside the Phone app. Its support page gives three ways in, so pick the one that matches where the number is. These steps apply to Galaxy phones running Android 9.0 or later.</p>
<p>Typing the number in yourself is the most direct way. Tap More options, the three vertical dots, then tap Settings. Tap Block numbers, type the phone number, and tap Add, the plus sign. This works whether or not the number is saved in your contacts. It is the best route for a number you only saw on a bill or a letter.</p>
<p>Recents offer another way in. Select the number you want to block. Tap Menu, then tap Block, and tap Block again to confirm. You can also pick numbers from Recents or Contacts on the Block numbers screen, so you can add several numbers in one visit.</p>
<p>Menus can vary by carrier, software version, and phone model, Samsung warns. One label on your phone may read slightly differently. Look for the words Block numbers in the Phone app settings.</p>
<ol class="steps"><li>Open the Samsung Phone app.</li><li>Choose More options, the three vertical dots, then Settings.</li><li>Open Block numbers.</li><li>Type the phone number, then tap Add, the plus sign.</li></ol>
<div class="note"><b>Check this:</b> These menus can vary by carrier, software version, and phone model, so a label may read slightly differently on your phone.</div>
<h2>Block a number from contacts and messages</h2>
<p>A block in the Phone app stops calls from that number. It does not always stop texts, because texts arrive through your messaging app. To stop both, block the number in both places. That takes a few extra taps, and it is worth doing when the same number calls and texts you.</p>
<p>Saved contacts work a little differently on a Samsung phone. In the Contacts app, tap the contact you want to block. Tap More, then tap Block contact, and confirm by tapping Block. Blocked contacts stay in your Contacts list, so you keep the name and number for later.</p>
<p>Next, block the same number in your messaging app. Touch and hold the conversation in Samsung Messages, tap More, then Block, and confirm. In Google Messages, touch and hold the conversation and tap Block. Find the list later in Samsung Messages under More options, then Settings, then Block numbers and spam.</p>
<p>Think of the Phone app and the Messages app as two separate doors. Most people only need the Phone app block. Add the Messages block when texts from the same number keep arriving after the calls stop.</p>
<h2>Block unknown or private numbers</h2>
<figure class="fig"><img src="https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=Phone+screen+showing+unknown+caller+warning&amp;w=560&amp;h=380&amp;c=7&amp;rs=1&amp;p=0&amp;o=5&amp;pid=1.7" alt="Phone screen showing unknown caller warning" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>
<p>Some calls show no number at all, just Private number or Unknown. You cannot block those one by one, because there is no number to add to a list. The switch is the only way to stop them. Google and Samsung both offer one switch that blocks them all, and it lives next to the blocked numbers list.</p>
<p>Pixel owners find the switch in the same place. In the Phone app, go to Settings, then Blocked numbers, and turn on Unknown. Calls from numbers that are not stored in your contacts still get through.</p>
<p>Galaxy phones do it a little differently. On the Block numbers screen, turn on the switch next to Block calls from unknown numbers. Samsung labels similar features Smart Call. Availability varies by model, carrier, and software version.</p>
<p>Weigh the trade-off before you turn this switch on. Doctors, schools, delivery drivers, and banks often call from numbers you do not know. Leave the switch off if you are waiting for a call from someone whose number you do not have. Turn it on when the unknown calls are mostly spam.</p>
<h2>What happens when you block a number</h2>
<p>Blocked callers hear nothing that tells them they were blocked. With the Phone app by Google, the call is declined on its own and never rings. With a Samsung phone, blocked calls go straight to voicemail.</p>
<p>Voicemail is the one loose end. Depending on your carrier and whether you use visual voicemail, blocked callers may or may not be able to leave voicemails. Visual voicemail shows your voicemails as a list inside the Phone app. Check your carrier or visual voicemail app for how it works on your phone.</p>
<p>One number is all a block covers. Calls and texts from any other number still reach you, which is why blocking one spam number rarely ends spam calls for good. That block also stays on your phone. It does not block the person inside a chat app, which has its own block list.</p>
<p>Your carrier can help where your phone cannot. Major US carriers offer network-level spam tools. Names and availability vary, so check your carrier&#x27;s support pages for activation steps. T-Mobile, Verizon and AT&amp;T each document their spam-filter offerings on their sites. T-Mobile customers can turn on Scam Block by dialing #662#, though dial codes can change, so check T-Mobile&#x27;s support page first. Verizon offers Call Filter, and AT&amp;T offers ActiveArmor.</p>
<p>Unblocking is as quick as blocking. Open the blocked numbers list in your Phone app settings. Choose Clear next to the number, then Unblock. Galaxy owners open the Block numbers screen and tap the red minus icon beside the number. Calls ring again at once.</p>

<h2>Myths you can ignore about blocking numbers</h2>
<p>Simple tools attract wrong ideas, and blocking is no exception. Four wrong ideas come up again and again. Knowing what a block cannot do matters as much as knowing how to set one. Each one can lead you to block the wrong thing.</p>
<p>First up is the myth that blocking a number stops all spam calls. It does not. Scammers call from many numbers, and a new one appears as soon as you block the old one. Turn on your phone spam filter and your carrier spam filter to catch the numbers you never see.</p>
<p>Another myth says blocking deletes the contact. That is wrong too. A blocked contact stays in your Contacts list. Name, number, and message history stay where they were. Only the calls and texts stop, so you can unblock later without losing anything.</p>
<p>The third myth says you cannot unblock a number once it is blocked. You can, any time, from the same list where you blocked it. Those steps are in the section above, and they take less than a minute. Nothing about a block is permanent.</p>
<ul><li>Blocked callers hear a warning: no, the call is declined or sent to voicemail, with no message about the block.</li><li>One block stops all spam: no, scammers use many numbers, so use the spam filter and carrier tools too.</li><li>Blocking deletes the contact: no, the contact stays in your list and you can unblock it any time.</li><li>Blocking stops every app: no, a chat app has its own block list, set inside that app.</li></ul>
<h2>Quick answers</h2>
<h3>Will the blocked number know I blocked it?</h3>
<p>No. The call is declined or sent to voicemail, and the caller hears nothing that mentions a block. They may guess, if every call goes straight to voicemail, but your phone sends no message telling them. Visual voicemail stops blocked callers leaving messages.</p>
<h3>Does blocking a number block texts too?</h3>
<p>Not on its own. To stop texts as well, block the same number in your messaging app. Touch and hold the conversation in Google Messages or Samsung Messages, then tap Block.</p>
<h3>Can I block a number that is not in my contacts?</h3>
<p>Yes. Block from your call history, or type the number into the Block numbers screen, as the steps above show. Neither route needs the number saved first.</p>
<h3>How do I unblock a number on Android?</h3>
<p>Find the blocked numbers list where you set the block, and remove the number there. You will find the steps in the section above, under What happens when you block a number.</p>
<h3>Why do I still get spam calls after blocking numbers?</h3>
<p>Spam callers use many numbers, so blocking one only stops that one. Turn on your phone spam filter and your carrier spam tool as well. With the Phone app by Google, the filter lives under More, then Settings, then Caller ID and spam.</p>
